---

FinFlick is a free Android app for Indian markets.

- **Swipe feed** — one story per card, LIVE every 15 seconds while the market is open
- **Deep Read** — swipe left for the full story, with every piece of jargon explained
- **Ask** — ask a question and get a sourced answer, or type a company to open its stock page
- **Markets** — India, mutual funds, bonds, IPOs, unlisted, crypto and the US
- **Screener** — 39 metrics and 6 ready-made screens over the NSE universe
- **Stock pages** — 20 sections per stock, every number with its source
- **Alerts and read-aloud** — for your watchlist and trending topics

Facts, never advice.

**Download:** get the Android app from the [website](https://tanishkbisht584-ux.github.io/finflick/#get).
