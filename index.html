Why a deposit-rate hike is really a margin story

When a bank raises what it pays savers, its cost of funds rises before it can re-price the loans it has already given. The gap between the two is the net interest margin — the number the street watches most.

Lenders with a large share of low-cost current and savings accounts feel this least.

Net interest marginWhat a bank earns on loans minus what it pays on deposits, as a share of its assets.
