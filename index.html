What it does

Eight things.
One swipe.

  1. 01

    A feed you flick throughOne story, one swipe

    Vertical cards, one story each, infinite. LIVE mode refreshes every 15 seconds while the market is open; switch it off for a calmer read. Tap IMPACT or SHORT / LONG to filter.

    News
  2. 02

    Swipe left for the full storyDeep Read

    The same story as a short newspaper piece — a few serif pages, then a glossary page for every term you just read. Nothing invented, nothing padded.

    Deep Read
  3. 03

    Ask anything, get sourcesWhy is the NIFTY moving?

    One box, two behaviours: type a question and get a sourced answer; type a company and land on its stock page. Try “Why is the NIFTY moving today?”

    Ask
    Why is the NIFTY moving today?
  4. 04

    Seven markets, 32 ledgersIndia to the US

    India, mutual funds, bonds, IPOs, unlisted, crypto and the US — each a set of dense, bordered tables: sectors, flows, OI trends, positioning, calendar, moves, mood and more.

    Markets
    INDIAMFBONDSIPOUNLISTEDCRYPTOUS
  5. 05

    39 metrics, six ways inScreens

    A screener over the quoted NSE universe. Start from a preset or build your own, fifty rows at a time.

    Screens
    VALUECOMPOUNDERSDIVIDENDGROWTHDEBT-FREE SMALLCAPPROMOTER HEAVY
  6. 06

    Twenty sections per stockEvery number, sourced

    Overview to documents: vitals, delivery & volume, F&O, corporate actions, seasonality, fundamentals, technicals, peers, earnings, pros & cons — every number with its source.

    Stock page
    Overview01Vitals03F&O05Fundamentals10Technicals11Documents20
  7. 07

    Hear it before you read itAlerts, read aloud

    Alerts for your watchlist and for trending topics. Turn on read-aloud and the biggest stories are spoken to you.

    Alerts
  8. 08

    Tap any word you don’t knowGlossary, everywhere

    Jargon is underlined everywhere in the app. Tap it and get a one-line, plain-English definition — right there, without leaving the story.

    Glossary
    The RBI left the repo rate unchanged.
    Repo rateThe rate at which the RBI lends money to banks. When it rises, loans get costlier.
